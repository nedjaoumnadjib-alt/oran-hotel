About this hotel

Perched above the Front de Mer promenade, Le Panorama Oran looks straight out over the Mediterranean toward the Santa Cruz hillside. Rooms are done in warm plaster tones with brass fittings, and the rooftop pool stays open until midnight through the summer season.

The hotel sits an easy walk from Place du 1er Novembre and the Grande Poste, with the train station a short taxi ride away — a comfortable base whether you're here for the coastline or the old town.
